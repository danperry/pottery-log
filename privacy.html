Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: Pottery Log has no server and no accounts of its own. Your pottery log is stored on your device and, if you turn on backup, in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives your data.

Pottery Log ("the app") is a web app for keeping a log of pottery pieces. This policy explains what data the app handles and how, including the data it accesses when you connect your Google account.

Data you enter

The pieces you log (titles, photos, clay bodies, forming methods, glazes, firing details, stages, dates and notes) are stored in your browser's storage on your device (IndexedDB). They are not sent to the developer or to any third party. Clearing your browser's data for the app or uninstalling it deletes them from the device.

The app also stores a few settings in your browser: your backup folder, whether automatic backup is on, when you last backed up, and a random identifier for the device. That identifier is used to warn you before one device overwrites another's backup.

Google account and Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file lets the app see, create and change only the Google Drive files and folders that it created, or that you explicitly choose with Google's folder picker. The app cannot see or open any other files in your Drive.

With that permission, the app:

  • reads your Google account's name and email address from the Drive API, only to show which account is connected;
  • creates a backup folder, or uses the folder you pick;
  • writes your pottery log (pottery-log.json) and your photos (as JPEG files) into that folder when you back up;
  • reads those files back when you restore;
  • deletes backed-up photos of pieces you have deleted from your log.

All of this happens directly between your browser and Google. The app has no server of its own, so the developer never receives your Google data, your files or your access token. The short-lived access token Google issues is kept in your browser's storage on your device so the connection survives restarts. It is deleted when you tap Sign out, and Google expires it after about an hour.

Limited Use disclosure

Pottery Log's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore features described above. It is not transferred to others, not used for advertising, not sold, and not read by humans. It is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer your data. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Services the app relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. GitHub may log standard web request information, such as IP addresses, when the app is loaded. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google provides sign-in, the folder picker and Google Drive, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Disconnect: tap Sign out on the app's Backup screen. You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete backups: delete the backup folder or its files in Google Drive.
  • Delete local data: delete pieces in the app, or clear the site's data in your browser settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
